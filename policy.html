Squat Form AI: Video Analysis — Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Squat Form AI: Video Analysis (the "Application"), provided by Pavlo Sabov (the "Service Provider"), handles information when you use it.

The Application uses your camera or a video you select to analyze squat sets. It does not require an account. Subscription purchases are processed through Apple and RevenueCat as described below.

Information Handled by the Application

Workout videos and results. When you record a set or choose a video, the Application processes the footage on your device to estimate repetitions, depth, and other movement measures. It saves workout dates, any weight you enter, analysis results, and retained set videos in the Application's local storage. The Application does not upload your workout footage or analysis results to the Service Provider or RevenueCat. Videos you select remain subject to your Photos and device backup settings; local app data may also be included in device backups.

App preferences. The Application stores settings and onboarding choices locally on your device.

Purchase and technical data. The Application uses Apple's purchase system and RevenueCat for subscription management and paywall presentation. These services may process:

  • An app-specific user identifier used to manage purchases
  • Purchase, subscription, and entitlement information
  • App and device information, such as app version, device model, and operating system
  • Network information, such as IP address, when communicating with the service
  • Paywall and purchase interaction events needed to operate or evaluate subscription offers

The Application does not ask you to create an account or provide a name, email address, or phone number to use its workout features. If you email the Service Provider for support, your message and email address will be used to respond to you.

The Application does not display advertisements, include an advertising SDK, or use your workout data to track you across other companies' apps or websites for advertising. The Service Provider does not sell your personal information.

Data Deletion

Uninstalling the Application removes its local data from the device. Copies may remain in device backups or in your Photos library according to your Apple settings. The Application may also remove older saved workouts and associated videos as its local history limit is reached.

Subscription data is managed by Apple and can be viewed or cancelled in your device's Settings under Apple ID > Subscriptions.

To request deletion of any technical data held by third-party services, contact the Service Provider at pasha.company.work@gmail.com.

Data Retention

The Service Provider does not operate a server for workout videos or analysis. Locally saved results and videos remain until they are removed by the Application or you uninstall it, subject to any device backups. Apple and RevenueCat retain purchase-related information according to their policies linked above. Support emails are retained as needed to respond to and follow up on your request.

Data Protection

Your locally stored data is protected by your device's security features and backup settings. The Service Provider does not have access to your on-device workout videos or results through the Application.
